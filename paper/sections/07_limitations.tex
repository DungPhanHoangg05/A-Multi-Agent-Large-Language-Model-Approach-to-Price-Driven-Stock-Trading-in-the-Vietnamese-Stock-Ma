\section{Limitations \& Future Work}
\label{sec:limitations_futurework}

\subsection{Limitations}

\paragraph{L1. Statistical scale and economic interpretation.}
The paired benchmark controls upstream reports and evaluates 180 decisions, but each symbol contributes only 20 observations. This limits per-symbol statistical power: no individual McNemar test is significant at 5\%, although the cross-symbol Wilcoxon test is significant. The nine tickers are also not a probability sample of the Vietnamese market, so the results should be interpreted as evidence for this benchmark universe rather than a market-wide estimate.

Directional skill does not directly imply profitability. The Full System improves mean accuracy by 13.9 pp and outperforms No-Alpha in account return for every symbol, yet its mean compounded return remains $-1.69\%$, with only three symbols positive. Because SHORT predictions map to CASH, correct bearish forecasts generate no trading return. The observed directional lift therefore does not establish deployable profitability.

\paragraph{L2. Simplified execution model.}
The fixed-cycle ledger aligns labels, alpha targets, and account returns on the same next-open-to-target-close net return and includes two-leg fees and slippage. However, these costs remain benchmark assumptions. The simulator omits dynamic spreads, taxes, order-book depth, partial fills, market impact, position limits, and cross-symbol capital competition. It also assumes each $S=L=3$ cycle is closed before the next entry. A venue-calibrated event-driven simulator is therefore required before live deployment.

\paragraph{L3. Alpha selection and sensitivity.}
Selecting five factors from 85 candidates introduces multiple-testing and winner's-curse risk. Although point-in-time cutoffs prevent future-bar leakage, the same trailing sample is used to estimate IC sign, compute oriented Accuracy and Sharpe, and rank the factors. Selecting large composite scores across many candidates can therefore produce optimistic diagnostics. No nested cross-validation, Deflated Sharpe Ratio, multiplicity correction, or sign-stability criterion was applied. The benchmark evaluates the complete adaptive policy at later cutoffs, but its per-factor statistics should not be interpreted as unbiased factor discoveries.

The 80 WorldQuant-derived entries are heuristic single-asset adaptations rather than exact implementations of the original cross-sectional formulas. Their stability was not evaluated through IC decay, lagged signal autocorrelation, or turnover. These diagnostics are needed to determine whether apparent predictiveness survives to the $L=3$ horizon and remains economically meaningful after trading costs.

\paragraph{L4. Hosted-model and visual reliability.}
Temperature zero and fixed prompts do not guarantee deterministic hosted-model outputs, and provider-side revisions or inference nondeterminism may still affect repeated runs. The paired design controls this variation within each Full/No-Alpha comparison, but not necessarily across independent executions.

The Pattern and Trend agents use deterministic OHLCV rules to detect obvious inconsistencies in visual conclusions. These rules check output consistency only and leave the VLM's actual use of the cited candle, wick, breakout, or trendline unverified. Prior work reports strong trend sensitivity and persistent spatial-reasoning limitations in financial chart understanding \cite{wang2026martingale,shu2026finchart}. Establishing causal visual grounding would require matched counterfactual charts and human-labelled local evidence, neither of which is included in this study.

\paragraph{L5. Sentiment coverage and attribution.}
Strict cutoff filtering prevents future and undated articles from entering historical decisions, but sentiment coverage remains sparse and uneven across firms. The three-symbol ablation also shows heterogeneous effects, including a positive independent contribution for VNM and a negative factor--sentiment interaction for VCB. These results do not support a broad causal claim about Vietnamese news sentiment. New records distinguish local Transformers, Dedicated Endpoint, and lexicon execution, but the model repository is not pinned to an immutable Hub commit and the historical caches remain \texttt{legacy-unknown}. Backend provenance therefore improves auditability without establishing bitwise reproducibility or retroactive scorer attribution.

\subsection{Future Work}

\paragraph{F1. Larger and longer evaluation.}
The most important extension is a preregistered study over a broader universe such as the VN100 and multiple market regimes. More test points would increase per-symbol power, support sector-level analysis, and permit out-of-time validation rather than repeated analysis of the same recent period.

\paragraph{F2. Stronger baselines and multiplicity control.}
Future experiments should add random-walk, logistic, tree-based, and sequence-model baselines on the identical information set. A time-ordered nested design should repeat normalisation, IC orientation, and top-five selection wholly inside each inner training fold, then score the frozen selected bundle on an untouched outer interval \cite{varma2006bias}. White's Reality Check, false-discovery-rate control, or the Deflated Sharpe Ratio should additionally account for searching across 85 candidates. Selection frequency, IC-sign agreement, and rank correlation across rolling calibration windows should be published rather than reporting only the winning factors.

\paragraph{F3. Execution and portfolio realism.}
A portfolio simulator should model lot sizes, taxes, dynamic spreads, liquidity limits, partial fills, and market impact, and should allocate capital jointly across symbols. Confidence gates and risk-based sizing can then be assessed without conflating forecasting accuracy with all-in binary allocation.

\paragraph{F4. Component reliability.}
Repeated hosted-model runs should quantify decision variance and archive provider revision metadata whenever available. ViSoBERT experiments should pin an exact Hub commit and test agreement between the local Transformers and Dedicated Endpoint paths before pooling their outputs. Vision faithfulness should be tested with two matched interventions: preserve global trend while changing only the local candlestick evidence, and preserve the local pattern while swapping the trend confounder. Directional changes should then be compared with rule-implied changes and human local-evidence annotations, rather than scored only against the future return. Sentiment should likewise be evaluated against a Vietnamese financial-domain test set. The observed negative VCB interaction also motivates an explicit conflict-resolution mechanism between factor and sentiment evidence.

\paragraph{F5. Per-factor translation audit.}
Each registered adaptation should be profiled by horizon-specific IC decay, autocorrelation at several lags, sign persistence, exposure turnover, and net performance after the same two-leg costs used by the account simulator. Diagnostics should be stratified by symbol and market regime and compared with simple price/volume baselines. Factors whose orientation changes frequently or whose apparent IC disappears before $L=3$ should be excluded by a preregistered stability rule rather than retained through ad hoc interpretation.

\subsection{Broader Impact}

Localized financial AI can broaden research beyond English-language, highly liquid markets, but automated use in a shallow market may amplify noise and herding. The framework should therefore be treated as decision-support research rather than trading advice. Reproducible releases must preserve model and data provenance while respecting news copyright, provider terms, and privacy obligations.
