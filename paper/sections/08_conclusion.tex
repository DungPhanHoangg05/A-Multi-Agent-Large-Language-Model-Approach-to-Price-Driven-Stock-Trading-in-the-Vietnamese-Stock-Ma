\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The new frozen common-snapshot evaluation completes all 474 planned research decisions, including 102 Base--Full pairs across nine Vietnamese equities, six historical blocks and twelve decision dates. Full achieves 62.75\% raw accuracy, below constant CASH at 75.49\%; its balanced accuracy is 48.31\% and MCC is $-0.035$, versus 55.61\% balanced accuracy for price-only logistic regression. Its mean net return over 51 independent symbol--block accounts is $-0.88\%$, below CASH at zero and price logistic at $-0.68\%$. The evidence therefore does not establish predictive superiority or profitability. The Full--Base balanced-accuracy difference is only 0.55 percentage points, with a descriptive six-block interval spanning zero.

Preselected three-repeat decision/factorial/placebo and upstream panels quantify variation only on their limited supports; their heterogeneous results do not establish isolated factor or sentiment value. Train-fitted majority, momentum, two logistic feature sets, factor voting and continuous holding provide stronger context than the weaker Base reports control. Both-class metrics, explicit common support and daily ledgers make the negative comparison inspectable. The new experiment remains historical and exploratory: provider revisions and model contamination are unverified, news availability and financial-domain sentiment validity are uncertain, and fractional immediate-cash ledgers do not certify settlement or deployability. The separate 180-decision legacy audit is preserved, with its 65.0\% Full accuracy below 68.9\% CASH; it is not pooled with the new study because authenticated historical inputs remain unavailable. A broader independent evaluation and validated execution/input provenance would be needed to support stronger claims.

The temporal audit documents 33 rising, 35 falling and 34 sideways individual price contexts, with train-calibrated descriptive volatility and turnover strata. It preserves calendar missingness and cross-sectional shocks in resampling. Twelve historical decision dates and six blocks provide limited evidence, not independent market replications or a contamination-free prospective test.
