\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We evaluated a specific multi-agent LLM implementation for Vietnamese equities on a frozen historical scope, with common-support controls, archived calls and explicit label/account contracts. On 102 units, Full BA is \FullBA\% versus Base \BaseBA\%; ordinary accuracy is strongly affected by class imbalance, a price-only logistic comparator has BA \LogisticPriceBA\%, and Full's mean account return is \FullReturn\%. Repeated panels reveal variability and do not establish stable component advantages. The contribution is a reproducible record of this implementation's observed behaviour and its limits, rather than evidence of causal alpha or deployable profitability. Native row-wise noncausality, in-sample selector diagnostics, missing financial-news/PIT validation and order/epoch confounding remain disclosed. A corrected prospective study and stronger independent validation are needed before restoring the claims those limitations currently prevent.
