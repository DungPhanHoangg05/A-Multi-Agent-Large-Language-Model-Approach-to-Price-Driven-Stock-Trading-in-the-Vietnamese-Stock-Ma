\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This study advances knowledge-based financial decision support through a five-agent architecture that fuses quantitative, textual, and visual evidence for Vietnamese equities. Its principal contributions are an auditable, point-in-time registry of 85 OHLCV alpha candidates; cutoff-controlled Vietnamese sentiment with explicit provenance; deterministic consistency gates for vision-language outputs; a shared-report protocol and four-way ablation for controlled component evaluation; and a horizon-aligned ledger with two-sided transaction costs. Together, these elements provide a reproducible basis for heterogeneous knowledge integration in an emerging, non-English market while keeping forecast evidence distinct from simulated execution.

The paired benchmark shows a significant directional improvement when structured alpha information is available, and the ablation identifies alpha factors as the more consistent independent contributor relative to sentiment. Sentiment effects and factor--sentiment interactions remain stock-dependent. Although the Full System improves account outcomes relative to its control, its negative mean net return supports forecasting and decision-support value only; deployable profitability remains unestablished. Broader out-of-time universes, multiplicity-aware factor selection, stronger non-LLM baselines, repeated model runs, and venue-calibrated portfolio simulation remain necessary for practical validation.
