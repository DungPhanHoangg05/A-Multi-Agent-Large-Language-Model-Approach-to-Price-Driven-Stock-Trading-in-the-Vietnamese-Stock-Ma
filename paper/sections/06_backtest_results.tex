\section{Backtest Results}

\subsection{New Common-Snapshot Baseline Comparison}
\label{sec:r1_common_results}

All 474 research decisions are complete, including all 102 main B/AS pairs,
the 18-unit decision panel and the nine-unit upstream panel. Ten smoke
decisions are excluded. The main support is 25 UP and 77 DOWN net outcomes;
all nine classifiers use exactly these 102 units, with no excluded prediction.
Tables~\ref{tab:r1_common_metrics} and \ref{tab:r1_common_classes} report the
pooled comparison and both classes. \ref{app:r1_common_detail}
reports equal-symbol macro results, every baseline for each symbol, and
block/regime strata; complete per-class/confusion counts for all 189 groups
are also available in the machine-readable release.

Full has TP/FN/TN/FP of 5/20/59/18 and Base 7/18/52/25.
Full's 62.75\% raw accuracy exceeds Base's 57.84\%, but is below CASH and
train-majority at 75.49\%. Its BA is 48.31\%, compared with Base at
47.77\%, CASH at 50.00\%, price logistic at 55.61\% and market-input

forecasting improvement nor superiority over these controls is established.
Full issues 23 LONG calls versus Base's 32. Its lower LONG frequency

identify useful factor information. Full UP precision/recall is
21.74\%/20.00\%, and DOWN precision/recall is 74.68\%/76.62\%.
Macro BA is 45.96\% for Full and 50.07\% for Base over eight defined
symbols, unlike their pooled weighting; the macro table reports all defined
denominators rather than filling undefined values with zero.

$0.00\%$ for CASH, $-0.68\%$ for price logistic, $-3.00\%$ for always-LONG
cycles and $-2.13\%$ for continuous hold. These are means over 51 separate
accounts, not a portfolio return. The small relative loss reduction over
Base establishes neither profitability nor isolated alpha value. Every
account has a daily mark-to-market ledger with costs, turnover, exposure
and daily-close drawdown; the same execution assumptions apply throughout.

\subsection{Legacy Nine-Symbol Paired Benchmark}

The original 180 net labels contain 56 UP and 124 DOWN outcomes. Full has
confusion counts TP/FN/TN/FP of 16/40/101/23, compared with 15/41/77/47 for
Base reports. Its 65.0\% accuracy is below constant DOWN/CASH at 68.9\%.
Full's balanced accuracy is 55.01\% and MCC is 0.113; the Base reports values
are 44.44\% and $-0.108$. Full issues 39 LONG calls versus 62 for Base reports.
Of 25 additional correct Full decisions, 24 are DOWN and one is UP. This
pattern is consistent with fewer LONG decisions in a predominantly
unprofitable-cycle sample, but does not identify the cause as alpha information.
Tables~\ref{tab:r1_legacy_metrics} and \ref{tab:r1_class_metrics} report the
imbalance-aware audit without suppressing the stronger constant baseline.

\subsection{Numerical Reconstruction and Daily Ledgers}

Tables~\ref{tab:r1_numeric_metrics} and \ref{tab:r1_comparability} report the
additional numerical study under Section~\ref{sec:r1_numeric_protocol}.
These results belong to the new snapshot and are not ranked against Full's
legacy outcomes. The price-only and factor-augmented logistic models answer
different information-set questions; neither has verified identical archived
Full reports. The continuous-hold account buys once and sells once, unlike
always-LONG cycles. Daily-close drawdown, turnover, fees and exposure are
retained per symbol in the reanalysis ledgers; no daily Full ledger is inferred
from mismatched reconstructed prices. The train-rate random control is not
represented as matching Full's exposure. Accordingly, the newly implemented
baselines alone do not establish a valid comparison with legacy Full.
The newly frozen study in Section~\ref{sec:r1_common_results} supplies the
actual common-snapshot LLM comparison, as historical exploratory evidence.
