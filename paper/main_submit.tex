%% Abstract
\begin{abstract}
We evaluate a five-agent decision-support architecture for Vietnamese equities using a new frozen common-snapshot study and a separate audit of legacy predictions. The new study contains 102 paired market units across nine equities, six historical blocks in 2023--2026 and twelve decision dates. Its net-cycle target has 25 UP and 77 DOWN outcomes. Full achieves 62.75\% accuracy, below constant DOWN/CASH at 75.49\%; balanced accuracy is 48.31\%, Matthews correlation is $-0.035$, and LONG frequency is 22.55\%. Base reports, which removes both factors and sentiment, achieves balanced accuracy of 47.77\%. Price-only logistic regression achieves 55.61\% balanced accuracy on the same support. Full's mean net return across 51 separate symbol--block accounts is $-0.88\%$, versus zero for CASH and $-0.68\%$ for price logistic regression. A six-block paired bootstrap yields a descriptive Full--Base balanced-accuracy interval of $[-5.91,8.00]$ percentage points. Sparse dates and shared market shocks limit inference. Three-repeat decision and upstream panels are reported on their preselected supports; their dispersion is not generalised to the full sample. All 474 planned research decisions are archived, excluding ten smoke decisions. The new evidence does not establish predictive superiority, isolated alpha/sentiment value or profitability. Historical-model contamination, uncertain news availability and simplified fractional immediate-cash execution limit interpretation. Legacy results remain separate because their authenticated input snapshot could not be recovered.
\end{abstract}
